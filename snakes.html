Snake Sightings Alert Campus During Rainy Season 🐍

In recent weeks, students and staff have reported several snake sightings around campus. High humidity and flooding during the rainy season force these animals to leave their natural habitats and seek drier refuge. Abundant vegetation and vacant university lots also offer them easy places to hide and find food.

Safety Guidelines: Do's and Don't's

Although these encounters can cause concern, remaining calm is essential to avoid accidents. Security personnel recommend following these strict protocols:

  • What to do: Maintain a safe distance, move back slowly, and immediately notify university security or a professor.
  • What NOT to do: Never try to touch, catch, or kill the animal, and do not approach it to take photographs.

Coexisting with Nature

While some species can be venomous, most snakes avoid human contact and only attack when threatened. Campus spaces were built over areas where these species lived long before us. Understanding that every living being holds a vital function in the ecosystem helps us coexist with respect and environmental responsibility.
