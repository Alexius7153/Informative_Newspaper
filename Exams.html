The evaluations are here.

Here's what you need to know about it.

Ordinary evaluation:

The main path, this depends on the performance you achieved during the course to be exempt from this exam.

To be exempt from the regular exam, you must obtain at least a grade of 8, given by the average of both the grades for partial exams 1 and 2. To be able to pass the regular exam, you must have at least an average of 6 between the first and second partial exams and the grade you get in the regular exam.

To take this exam, you are required to have 80% attendance for the semester.

Extraordinary evaluation:

If you have not managed to obtain the minimum to be exempt from the ordinary exam, you will face the extraordinary exam, which is your next opportunity.

In this exam, the minimum passing grade is again 6. The grade you achieve on this exam will be the one assigned to you for the semester exam, but as an extraordinary grade.

The requirement to take this exam is that you have a minimum of 60% attendance. You will not be able to take it if you do not meet the minimum attendance requirement and you will be given NE (No Entitlement) status as a consequence.

Proficiency certificate

Again, you can only pass if you achieve a grade of 6. For this exam, you will need a minimum of 30% of the total course attendance to be able to take the exam. You will fall directly to taking the proficiency certificate if for some reason you did not pass the extraordinary exam or did not take it.

This is your last chance to pass the semester. If you fail this exam, you must repeat the corresponding subject.

Our team wishes you the best of luck on these exams.
