End-of-semester assessments Are Here: Essential Guidelines 📝

As the evaluation period approaches, understanding the academic regulations is crucial for success. Here is everything you need to know about the grading system, attendance requirements, and exam categories for this semester.

1. Ordinary Evaluation (Regular Exam)

Your eligibility depends heavily on your performance throughout the course. Students can be exempt from this exam by achieving a minimum average score of 8.0, calculated from the first and second partial grades.

If you do not qualify for exemption, the minimum passing grade for the regular exam is 6.0 (averaged with your partial scores). To sit for this exam, you must fulfill a strict 80% attendance requirement for the semester.

2. Extraordinary Evaluation

If you fail to achieve the required scores during the ordinary phase, the extraordinary exam represents your next opportunity. The minimum passing grade remains 6.0, and the score achieved will be officially registered as your final extraordinary grade.

Candidates must maintain at least 60% attendance to be eligible. Failing to meet this threshold results in an immediate NE (No Entitlement) status, disqualifying you from the exam.

3. Proficiency Certificate

This evaluation is a student's final opportunity to pass the subject for the current semester. Students drop directly to this phase if they fail or miss the extraordinary exam. A minimum of 30% attendance is mandatory to take it.

The passing grade remains 6.0. Failing this final instance means you must repeat the entire subject next semester. Missing any scheduled evaluation without justification will result in an official NS (No Show) status.

Important Reminder: Always keep your student credential up to date. Forgetting your ID on exam day will severely complicate your entry and overall experience. The editorial team wishes you the best of luck on your upcoming tests!
